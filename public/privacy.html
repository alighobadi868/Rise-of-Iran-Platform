Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This is an initial operational privacy notice for the Rise of Iran Initiative website. Before public launch, the project must identify the responsible legal entity or person, confirm the applicable legal basis, and review this notice for the jurisdictions in which it operates.

Information submitted to us

If you use the contact form, the server stores the name, email address, subject, message, consent flag, and submission time in a local SQLite database. The service currently has no email delivery or automated deletion workflow configured. Do not send identity documents, passport or national ID numbers, financial details, health information, or confidential material through this site.

Purpose and access

Contact information is intended only to respond to inquiries and manage project communications. Database access should be restricted to authorized maintainers. The hosting operator may process technical logs as part of normal service operation.

Newsletter

The newsletter endpoint can store an email address, but confirmation email and unsubscribe workflows are not yet enabled. It must not be presented as an active mailing list until those workflows and a compliant consent process are implemented.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. The service worker may cache static website assets on your device for offline access. The hosting environment may maintain security logs.

Your choices

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests, email new.persia.rise@gmail.com. Do not include sensitive identity data in your initial request. A response process and retention schedule must be established before the site is used at scale.

Security and scope

Reasonable technical controls are included in the codebase, but no website can guarantee absolute security. The site now supports password-protected user accounts and manual profile-review requests. It stores account name, email address, password hash and salt, session-token hashes, and the profile details and explanation submitted for review. Automatic email verification is not configured. The review process is not connected to a government-ID verification provider and is not official identity verification. The site does not request or store government ID numbers or images of identity documents. A formal identity-verification provider must not be enabled without a separate legal, privacy, security, retention, and deletion review.
