Research assistant

A no-API-key research assistant that runs a compact language model directly in your browser to explore civic, policy, Iran, and international-relations questions. AI responses can be wrong; verify important claims using primary sources.

Service statusChecking browser support…The first use downloads the model from its public model host. Your question is processed locally in this browser and is not sent to an AI API.

The model requires a recent WebGPU-compatible browser and several hundred megabytes of initial downloads. Your prompt stays in this browser, but model files are downloaded from public hosting. Do not enter passwords, identity numbers, confidential documents, or sensitive personal information.
