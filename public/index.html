01 / OUR MISSION

A serious project for Iran's future

We are building an independent initiative for research, public dialogue and civic participation. We distinguish the work already completed from the work still ahead.

⌕

Research with evidence

Explore public policy, institutions and society through careful questions, credible sources and transparent reasoning.

◎

Open civic dialogue

Make room for constructive criticism, informed debate and contributions from people with different expertise.

⌘

Responsible participation

Offer a clear route to submit ideas, express interest and take part in a growing research community.

02 / GUIDING PRINCIPLES

Modern in method. Rooted in heritage.

Persian history can inspire a forward-looking civic culture, while institutions and public decisions must be judged by evidence and accountability.
