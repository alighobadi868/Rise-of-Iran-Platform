Rise of Iran Initiative is an independent civic-research initiative in formation. It aims to develop useful analysis, strengthen civic knowledge, and create space for informed public participation.

Explore the initiative ↗Research priorities

Current status: initiative in formation · not yet incorporated

01 / Mission

Build the foundations before making promises.

Lasting progress requires credible research, capable civic institutions, public trust, and transparent decision-making. Our work is intended to contribute to that foundation through documented analysis and open, constructive discussion.

Rise of Iran is independent and nonpartisan. It does not claim to represent all Iranians, a political party, a government, or any person or organization that has not formally joined it.
